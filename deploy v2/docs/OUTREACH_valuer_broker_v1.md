# Outreach — First Valuer / Broker Contact (v1)

> **Date:** 2026-06-20 · **Type:** Copy-ready outreach for the board's NOW move
> (`docs/BOARD_DECISION_v2_post_deliberation.md`): *get one valuer or broker to hand you their next
> three valuation sheets.* Pairs with the one-pager `docs/DEMO_institutional_onepager_TD93317_v1.html`.

## The strategy in one line
You are **not** selling a product and you are **not** asking for a data dump. You are offering a
licensed professional a free, fast **second opinion** that *makes them look good* — and the
data-capture (real figures on properties they already know) is the natural byproduct of them
testing it. Frame everything as *helping them*; the flywheel turns by itself.

## Who to send to (priority order)
1. **A licensed valuer you already know warmly** — they feel the manual-labour pain most, they
   hold certified sheets, and a co-sign from them is credibility gold.
2. **An active villa/land broker** — prices properties daily, has confirmed sale prices, fast to test.
3. *(Not yet: banks — that's the LATER stage, post-license. Don't open a 12-month procurement now.)*

## The honest framing (say this, mean it)
- Free. Independent (MoJ open data). **Not** a certified valuation — a cross-check that *complements*
  the licensed valuer, never replaces them. (This also disarms the "are you competing with me?" fear.)
- Lead with the proof, not the pitch: *reproduced a certified bank valuation to ±0.35% in seconds.*
- The ask is tiny: *a few minutes on 2–3 properties you already know* + your professional read.

## After they reply
- **In the session:** open the tool on *their* property; let them say "that's close." Then ask if
  they'd share the actual figure / sheet so you can "tighten it" — that line *is* the data capture.
  Each documented figure → `validate_gt_sheet.py` → `VALIDATION_LOG.md`.
- **No reply in ~3 days:** ONE gentle nudge ("just floating this back up — happy to come to you").
  Then leave it; move to the next name.

## Don't
- Don't ask for a bulk data export — ask them to *test it on properties they know*.
- Don't oversell ("most accurate in Qatar") — you have one calibration point; the ±0.35% example is
  honest and strong on its own.
- Don't pitch a paid product — it's a free cross-check until the license lands (Decision 28/2023).
