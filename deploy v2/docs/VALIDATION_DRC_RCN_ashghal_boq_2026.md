## 5. The cross-check result (CONFIRMS the ladder — does NOT change it)
- The all-in build-up (~2,500 central; band ~2,200 ordinary → ~3,500 luxury) **falls inside the
  existing §20.9 RCN ladder** (shell 1,200 · ordinary 2,200 · good 2,500 · high 3,000 · luxury 3,500).
- The **structure (~870 QAR/m²)** was built from **independent Ashghal unit rates** and lands on a
  ladder whose sole prior anchor is the **TD-93317** bank report (~3,000 high, ±1 %). **Two
  independent, different-nature sources (a government BoQ on the structure + a certified bank report
  on the all-in) triangulate on the same ladder** → classic RICS triangulation: **confidence in the
  ladder rises; no value-affecting change is justified.**

## 6. DRC reconciliation + E25 (supporting, not calibrating)
- DRC(new) ≈ **land** (Abu Hamour ~3,800 QAR/m² × 450 ≈ **1.71M**) **+ building** (~1.1–1.7M) ≈
  **2.9–3.4M**, vs the **AVM market value 2.5M** (56/565/10, comparison_bracket).
- DRC > market on an existing villa **supports E25** (cost is a floor; the market sits below
  replacement cost) — recorded as **SUPPORTING the under-anchor direction (B-2), NOT a calibration
  point.** 56/565/10 is an existing villa, not a documented new-stock sale → it does **not** unlock
  the B-2 `luxury_new` n≥20 (which stays PARKED on **documented** new-stock sales).
