# Board Decision — v2, Post-Deliberation (decision of record)

> **Date:** 2026-06-20 · **Type:** Strategic decision of record. **Supersedes** the *prescription*
> in `docs/BOARD_REVIEW_business_panel_v1.md` (the v1 diagnosis stands; its directed move is
> sharpened here). **Drives** `docs/PLAN_first_flywheel_turn_90day.md`.
> **Method:** the five-seat operator board (Bezos / Musk / Zuckerberg / Buffett / Nadella) run
> through four deliberation loops — opening motions → cross-examination → stress-test against
> Thammen's real constraints → convergence + vote.

## The four loops (how the decision moved)

**Loop 1 — Opening motions (disagreement).** Bezos: customer with the most *frequent* need.
Musk: proprietary data or die. Zuckerberg: growth first, monetize later. Buffett: the
Decision-28/2023 license *is* the moat. Nadella: B2B wedge (banks/valuers), the ±0.35% proof is
the door. → Two fault lines: B2C-growth vs B2B-wedge; moat-first vs growth-first.

**Loop 2 — Cross-examination.** Bezos to Nadella: a *bank* is a 9–18 month procurement; a solo,
no-revenue, no-license founder starves first. Nadella to Zuckerberg: consumer growth needs a
budget + team a solo founder doesn't have. Zuckerberg to Buffett: license-first is death by
bureaucracy. Buffett to all: traction on public data is *renting* users on a copyable foundation.
**Musk (the pivot):** stop ranking paths by revenue/channel — rank by **how much data you'd OWN
per week** that nobody else has.

**Loop 3 — Stress-test against the real constraints** (solo operator · can't charge pre-license ·
stale public-data dependency · n=1 calibration · old data feeds closed · no marketing budget).
Re-scored as *data-captured-per-week × solo-feasible × free-is-fine*:
- **Banks:** high value, 9–18mo → **demote to "later."**
- **Consumer growth:** needs budget/team → **demote to "passive funnel."**
- **Licensed valuers + active brokers** (already hold certified sheets + confirmed prices): 5–10
  real figures in **weeks**, warm-intro reachable solo, free cross-check (license not a blocker)
  → **wins all three tests.**
- **License inquiry:** free + slow → **start the clock now, don't block.**
- Buffett's moat worry **collapses into Musk's reframe**: capturing proprietary outcome data *is*
  building the moat → growth-vs-moat was a false choice. Zuckerberg concedes: the "network" is the
  *data* network; fastest nodes are valuers, not consumers.

**Loop 4 — Convergence + vote.**

## The resolution (vote 5 / 5)

> **Make capturing proprietary property-outcome data the one priority — seed it from the people who
> already hold it (licensed valuers + active brokers), free, this month. That data is the accuracy
> unlock, the moat, and the proof for the license and the future bank deal at once.**

### The sequence

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| **NOW** | Get real figures flowing — one valuer/broker hands you their next 3 sheets. Target 10 documented in 30 days. |
| **PARALLEL** | File the Aqarat (Decision-28/2023) inquiry day 1 (start the slow clock). Keep the consumer tool live as the always-on capture net + brand. |
| **LATER** | The bank / enterprise revenue deal — once you hold the data moat + a license path, **not** before. |
| **STOP** | Engine micro-sprints for an audience of zero. Bank that energy into data capture. |

### Recorded dissents
- **Buffett (partial):** get a non-binding LOI / willingness-to-pay from at least one institution
  *even pre-license* — prove the money is real, or you're building a beautiful charity.
- **Musk (more aggressive):** hand-fed sheets are too slow at scale — in parallel, build ONE
  automated proprietary data pipeline (listings + transactions, continuous). Valuers are the seed;
  the pipeline is the moat at scale.

## What changed, v1 → v2

1. **From channel to asset.** v1: "go B2B." v2: the priority is *owning data nobody else has*; the
   channel is a means. Banks demoted (too slow for a solo, no-revenue, no-license founder);
   **valuers/brokers promoted** as the fast, free, solo-reachable seed.
2. **The growth-vs-moat fight dissolved.** Capturing proprietary outcome data *is* the moat — so
   there was nothing to choose. Hence 5/5.
3. **License reframed from blocker to background clock.** File now (free, slow); operate as a free
   cross-check while it processes — it never gates the flywheel.

## The single governing metric

**Documented outcome figures captured — per week.** Every decision routes through it. It is at
once the accuracy unlock (breaks the parked n≥20), the moat (non-public, non-copyable), and the
credibility for both the license and the eventual bank deal. *If a week passes with zero new
figures, the plan is off-track regardless of any other activity.*

## The first move (this week)

**Get one valuer or broker to hand you their next three valuation sheets.** That is the flywheel's
first turn; everything else in the 90-day plan is downstream of it.

> The blunt one-liner the whole board would sign: **you don't have an accuracy problem or a product
> problem — you have a data-ownership problem, and the cure is three sheets, not three sprints.**
